### Title intro (2026-10-18)

From the exe (`Title\title.cpp`). Mode 0 (`title_task` 0x45b6f0) runs at every title entered from the warning screen or the story (opcode 0x96); mode 2 (0x45b720) is the way back from the extras, without the intro. States from black, with `Title.004`: 0-2000 ms background #0 cross-fades in; 2000-3000 nothing; 3000-5000 ripple under the logo (opacity 0 → 256, size 0 → 25 px); 5000-7000 logo #10 at (0, 171) fades in, ripple 25 → 50 px; 7000-8100 ripple off, COMPLETE #14 at (100, 383), the four words fade in 500 ms each, 0/200/400/600 ms late. **A click skips the effects (2026-10-17).** In the original a click (or Ctrl, or a key in mask `0x20ee007`) ending an effect or clickable wait sets `skip_state_b` (`0x51f82e`), and every later effect and clickable wait ends at once until a message, `0x68`, `0x69` or `0x91` clears it. CONscripter got `clickskip` (`getclickskip %v`): a click ending a `delay` or effect makes later ones end at once (`print 1`) until the next text, click wait or `mpegplay`. The converter writes it (`Script::click_skips_effects`); skip gates (`Op::IfSkipping`) also jump while `getclickskip` is 1 (601 gates). Unclickable waits (`0x66` high half 0, `wait`) unaffected. Checked with 1,500 auto-play pages: same pages and pixels, 87 pages with shorter waits. Not checked with a real mouse.
